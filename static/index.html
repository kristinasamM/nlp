Project Information

About VivaCraft

VivaCraft is an authentic, student-built study companion and oral exam (viva voce) preparation system. Designed by engineering students to make preparing for your project defense calm, organized, and effective.

Team Contributions

👩‍💻
Komal Reading notes & finding topics

Document ingestion, clean sentence boundary detection, Flesch readability, Easy Notes generation with page references, and TF-IDF / TextRank keyphrase extraction.

👩‍💻
Anushka Creating questions & supported answers

Bloom's Taxonomy question synthesis, concise suggested answers, and semantic deduplication to avoid repetitive prompts.

👩‍💻
Kristina Practice feedback & revision suggestions

Multi-criteria answer assessment (concept recall, semantic cosine alignment, reasoning markers), what to add, and revision tracking.

👨‍💻
Parth Interface, navigation & downloads

Student-friendly study workflow, voice dictation, persistent revision storage, and publication-quality PDF exports.

Behind the scenes (NLP Pipeline Details) ▾

Offline & Deterministic: VivaCraft runs standard, explainable NLP algorithms (TF-IDF, PageRank graph centrality, Porter Stemming, and Cosine similarity). It requires no external API keys or cloud GPUs, ensuring complete privacy for your unpublished project notes.

Fair Scoring: Evaluates answers using stemmed concept matching so conversational spoken answers are graded fairly on meaning rather than rote textbook phrasing.
